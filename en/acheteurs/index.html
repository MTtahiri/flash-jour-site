How Flash-Jour works for buyers

  1. Apply — Submit your buyer profile: company name, products sought, estimated volumes, destination market.
  2. Get verified — Our team reviews your application within 48 hours. Only serious, documented importers are admitted.
  3. Post requirements — Once inside, post what you need: product, calibre, packing, incoterm (CIF/FOB), timeline.
  4. Receive proposals — Matched producers respond directly. Your identity remains confidential until you decide otherwise.
  5. Connect and negotiate — When you find the right match, connect directly. No commission charged on your transactions.

Why importers choose Flash-Jour

Flash-Jour was built by supply chain professionals who understand the friction in international fresh produce trade: unreliable suppliers, opaque pricing, and excessive intermediaries. Our network eliminates these pain points by connecting European buyers directly with certified exporters in Africa, South America, the Mediterranean, and beyond.

Whether you source mangoes from Senegal, citrus from Morocco, tomatoes from Spain, or avocados from Peru, Flash-Jour gives you access to producers who are actively exporting — not just listing speculatively. Every member has been vetted for real export capacity, valid phytosanitary documentation, and international trade experience.

The network is active with buyers based in France (Rungis), Belgium (Antwerp), the Netherlands (Rotterdam), Germany, and the UK. We serve importers, wholesale markets, foodservice distributors, and retail chains.

Frequently asked questions

What products are available?

Mangoes (Kent, Keitt, Tommy Atkins), citrus (clementines, oranges, lemons), tomatoes, avocados, grapes, peppers, courgettes, green beans and more — sourced from Morocco, Senegal, Peru, Spain, Turkey, Egypt and South Africa.

Is there a membership fee?

Access to Flash-Jour is currently free during our launch phase. We focus on building a trusted, curated network of verified professionals rather than maximising membership numbers.

Who can join as a buyer?

Importers, wholesale distributors, logistics operators, and retail buyers with a documented need for fresh produce imports are welcome to apply. We verify each application manually.

What incoterms are supported?

Most producers on Flash-Jour trade on FOB or CIF terms. Some also offer DAP or DDP for established relationships. Incoterm preferences can be specified in your requirement post.
