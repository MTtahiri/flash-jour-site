Frequently asked questions

Buyers & importers

How do I join Flash-Jour?

Fill in the qualification form. We review your application within 48h. Access is reserved for verified professionals only.

Why is my identity anonymous at first?

To protect your commercial confidentiality. Your details are only shared after mutual consent.

Is Flash-Jour free for buyers?

The first 20 founding members get free access. A subscription is offered thereafter.

Producers & exporters

What documents are required?

Export activity proof, available certifications (GlobalGAP, Organic, etc.).

Which product families are accepted?

Tropical fruits, citrus, stone fruits, fresh vegetables, aromatic herbs — global production.
