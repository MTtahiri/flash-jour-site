Simple. Secure. Qualified.

Flash-Jour is not an open marketplace. It's a private network where every member is vetted before accessing opportunities.

For buyers

  1. Apply — Submit your qualification form with professional documents.
  2. Validated — We review your profile within 48h.
  3. Access — Browse verified producer profiles matching your criteria.
  4. Post — Publish your buying needs anonymously.
  5. Receive — Get proposals from qualified producers.
  6. Connect — You decide if and when to share contact details.
